\section*{Code and data availability}
{\sloppy
PyGeoParallax-IN (version 0.3.0, MIT licence), with the scripts and case lists used in this study, is archived on Zenodo \citep{pygeoparallax}: \doi{10.5281/zenodo.23672246}. The concept DOI, \doi{10.5281/zenodo.23666957}, always resolves to the latest version. Development continues at \url{https://github.com/bapallinaveen60/PyGeoParallax-IN}. INSAT-3DR data are from MOSDAC (\url{https://www.mosdac.gov.in}), GPM 2A Ku data from NASA GES DISC \citep{gpm2aku_v08}, ERA5 from the Copernicus Climate Data Store \citep{c3s2018} and SRTM from OpenTopography.\par}
