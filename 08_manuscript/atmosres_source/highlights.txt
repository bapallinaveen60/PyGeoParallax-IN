- Open-source tool corrects INSAT-3DR imagery for cloud parallax on the ellipsoid
- Meteosat confirms the correction: corrected views of two satellites agree
- GPM radar can verify absolute cloud position only to about 3-5 km
- Coldest cloud tops sit 4-5 km west of monsoon rain cores, hiding parallax
- INSAT-3DR navigation errors are seasonal; near zero during the monsoon
