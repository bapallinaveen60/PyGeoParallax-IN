Weather satellites see tall storm clouds displaced from their true position, by up to 10 km over north-east India for the Indian satellite INSAT-3DR. We built a free tool that corrects this and tested it against 38 overpasses of a radar in space. The tool works as geometry predicts, but the radar can confirm positions only to about 3-5 km. Storms do not lean enough to explain why, so the cause lies above the radar's view or in the satellite's navigation.
